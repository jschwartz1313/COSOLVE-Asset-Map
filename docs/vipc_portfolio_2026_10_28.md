# VIPC Portfolio Update

Source: [VIPC portfolio](https://vipc.org/portfolio/), reviewed October 28, 2026.

## Scope

- 39 entries in VIPC's Autonomous Systems (26) or Aerospace (13) buckets.
- 19 new organization records; 20 existing records receive affiliation metadata.
- Includes William & Mary, Old Dominion University, Virginia Commonwealth University,
  and SURA's listing on behalf of Jefferson Lab. The existing Jefferson Lab record
  represents that last entry, rather than adding a duplicate laboratory or a supposed
  Virginia headquarters for SURA.
- Electra and Tidal Flight are represented by their existing documented Virginia-site
  records. The tag identifies the represented organization, not an award to that site.

## Data Readiness

Eight new records have a published business address positioned with the U.S. Census
address-range geocoder. These are site/address references, not exact suites, surveyed
facility footprints, manufacturing plants or operating-area boundaries.

Eleven new records do not have a verified map position. No city-center coordinates
were added to simulate a precise location. They remain available in the directory
and map result list without a point geometry.

LEAF, Counter-Drone Research Corporation and Eksdyne have no confirmed current direct
company website in this release. Their main website fields remain empty; VIPC is an
evidence/inquiry source, not a substitute company homepage. Counter-Drone Research
Corporation and Eksdyne descriptions distinguish historical research awards from
current service availability. Cowden's prototype history is likewise qualified.

Polaris's current official contact address in Alexandria takes precedence over the
older Arlington locality in VIPC's portfolio entry. No private residential addresses
from business registries were used to locate it.

## Tag Behavior

The `vipc_portfolio_sector` field powers the public badge, portfolio-only filter,
saved/shared map links, API output and CSV exports. It is separate from strategic
categories and does not change capability counts, editorial verification or ownership.

Portfolio membership can reflect a grant recipient; it does not necessarily mean an
equity investment, endorsement, current business availability or operational approval.

`apply_vipc_portfolio` attaches the specific public VIPC source without replacing
existing asset profiles or source reviews. It preserves private/archived records,
removed/rejected evidence and tags cleared after this update. Replaying the release
does not duplicate sources, comments or asset history.
