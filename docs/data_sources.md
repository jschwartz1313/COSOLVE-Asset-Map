# Real-data sources and methodology

The checked-in catalog contains 425 real, publicly documented Virginia ecosystem assets reviewed on August 5, 2026. It is generated by `scripts/build_real_asset_catalog.py` and stored in `data/virginia_real_assets.json` so changes can be reviewed before they reach the database.

## Coverage

| Source group | Records | Location treatment |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| FAA and Virginia Department of Aviation public-use airports | 64 | Published airport reference coordinates |
| Virginia Military Factbook installations | 30 | Generalized locality coordinates |
| Virginia public and private nonprofit degree-granting institutions, plus ECPI University | 82 | NCES IPEDS campus addresses and coordinates |
| Curated research, workforce, company, public-safety, federal, program, and infrastructure assets | 249 | Official public addresses, named sites, or generalized locality coordinates |

The catalog currently covers 12 Virginia ecosystem regions and six record types. Every record has an unmanned-systems relevance statement, at least one public evidence URL, a catalog review date, and normalized taxonomy values. Source verification status is tracked separately so staff can record human review and later rechecks.

## Inclusion rules

- The named organization, facility, program, or infrastructure asset must be real and publicly documented.
- The record must have direct unmanned-systems relevance or a clear enabling role defined by the project specification, such as aviation infrastructure, defense customer access, workforce, ports, advanced manufacturing, or test logistics.
- Higher-education coverage includes active Virginia public and private nonprofit degree-granting institutions in the NCES IPEDS 2024 directory, plus ECPI University in alignment with SCHEV statewide completion reporting. Institution-level inclusion identifies potential workforce capacity; it does not by itself claim a documented unmanned-systems program.
- Eastern Virginia Medical School is not represented as a separate institution because it became the Macon & Joan Brock Virginia Health Sciences at Old Dominion University in 2024.
- Public descriptions must not include classified, controlled, live-operational, vulnerability, or security-procedure information.
- Military and federal records identify only publicly documented installations and use generalized points.
- Airport inclusion does not imply permission to fly UAS there. Operators remain responsible for all airport, airspace, and regulatory authorization.

## Principal sources

- Federal Aviation Administration Airports Feature Service
- Virginia Department of Aviation Public-use Airport Directory
- Virginia Secretary of Veterans and Defense Affairs Military Factbook
- National Center for Education Statistics IPEDS 2024 institutional directory
- State Council of Higher Education for Virginia statewide completion reporting
- Virginia Economic Development Partnership unmanned-systems industry profile
- Virginia Innovation Partnership Corporation Unmanned Systems Center
- Official university, NASA, Port of Virginia, and research-laboratory pages

One authoritative overview may support multiple related records, such as a university, its laboratory, and a documented program. Public profile pages expose the source used for each individual record.

## Update workflow

```bash
python scripts/build_real_asset_catalog.py
git diff -- data/virginia_real_assets.json
python manage.py seed_real_data --prune
python manage.py test tests.backend.test_real_catalog
```

Review generated changes before seeding. The `--prune` option removes only catalog-managed records absent from the new snapshot; it does not remove manually curated records without the catalog provenance marker.

Production deployments use `python manage.py seed_real_data --add-missing`. That mode creates newly cataloged records and relationships without overwriting staff edits or verification work on records already in the database.

## Geographic reference layers

Virginia county and independent-city boundaries are derived from the U.S. Census Bureau
TIGERweb State and County service, January 1, 2025 vintage. The locally stored GeoJSON is
simplified for web display and should not be used for surveying or legal boundary decisions.

The ecosystem-region shading is a COSOLVE analytical layer built by grouping those localities
into the 12 regions used by the asset catalog. The regions support visualization and filtering;
they are not legal boundaries or official planning-district definitions. Regenerate the dissolved
display polygons with `python3 scripts/build_region_boundaries.py` after changing a locality
assignment.
