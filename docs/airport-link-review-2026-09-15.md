# Airport link follow-up - September 15, 2026

Revisited all ten airports left unresolved by the September 6 review. Two now
have better primary and contact links:

- **Falwell (W24):** [McLaren Corporation](https://mclarencorporation.com/) explicitly
  identifies its Falwell Airport location, public phone, and address. The
  [DOAV sponsor directory](https://doav.virginia.gov/airport_sponsors/) independently
  identifies Roger Beck of McLaren as airport manager. This is not Freedom
  Aviation's separate LYH facility.
- **Middle Peninsula Regional (FYJ):** the
  [Town of West Point airport page](https://www.west-point.va.us/community/visitors/airport.php)
  identifies the airport, address, facilities, and manager contact. The airport's
  separate `fly-fyj.com` domain was not confirmed usable, so use the working
  dedicated government page instead.

Eight retain their existing DOAV references: Chase City Municipal, Lake Anna,
Lake Country Regional, Mc Laughlin Seaplane Base, New London, New Market,
Smith Mountain Lake, and Tangier Island. This is not a claim that they lack a
website or are closed. See the dated manifest for each research decision.

Additional checks included the [Town of Clarksville facilities page](https://clarksvilleva.org/facilities/),
[Liberty's airport ownership article](https://www.liberty.edu/news/2016/01/06/liberty-acquires-local-airport/),
[Liberty's current mission-aviation initiative](https://www.liberty.edu/development-office/dc-8-gifted-to-liberty-university/),
and [SML Aviation](https://www.smlaviation.com/). These do not establish a new
dedicated airport-operator website: Clarksville links onward to AirNav; Liberty's
pages describe ownership/programs; SML Aviation is a tenant flight school.

The catalog now has 56 airport/operator-specific primary links out of 64 airport
records. Existing FAA evidence is retained. Deployment uses the same guarded
correction mechanism as the prior review, preserving staff changes, private or
rejected sources, and all editorial verification statuses. Repeated deployments
do not duplicate sources or history entries.
