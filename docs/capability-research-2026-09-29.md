# Practical-Use Profile Pass: September 29, 2026

## Scope

This pass expands 29 existing profiles and adds five distinct facilities. It does
not represent a fresh review of every asset in the inventory. The purpose is to
explain what a user can investigate, develop, test, or obtain from each resource,
rather than repeat capability tags in prose. There are no new interface controls.

Every changed profile has public operator or government evidence attached in
`data/capability_profiles_2026_09_29.json`. New facilities and their evidence are
in `data/asset_expansion_2026_09_29.json`.

## Interpretation Rules

- Published research examples establish an illustrated use, not commercial
  availability or a guaranteed operating limit.
- Outdoor acreage is not a flight boundary; building area and road-course lengths
  are not runway specifications.
- Langley CERTAIN, the historical UAS range entry, and ROAM are related resources,
  not three independently bookable airfields. ROAM's multi-vehicle supervision is
  research, not proof of unrestricted operational readiness.
- Longbow's NASA agreement is dated 2021. Unsupported present-day activity and
  partnership claims are cleared, not replaced with an assertion that it is closed.
- Counter-UAS research does not grant private jamming or mitigation authority.
- The Navy's Outdoor Autonomy Lab describes ground, surface, and underwater
  work. External participation requires government sponsorship; it is not labeled
  a public aerial-drone range.
- Review dates on specifications mean the evidence was checked. Historical source
  dates remain explicit where current configuration or access is not confirmed.
- New listings remain source-backed, with editorial review pending. No record or
  source is automatically promoted to verified by this pass.

## Safe Deployment

The release process adds missing records and applies corrections only to matching
catalog-generated baselines. Known older generated variants are accepted for five
profiles. Those variants come from earlier catalog revisions or the existing
profile-enrichment constants, not arbitrary live database values. Human edits,
assigned reviews, private records, and deliberately hidden/rejected sources remain
protected. An unexpected difference is logged for review rather than overwritten.

Remaining work includes broader inventory enrichment and direct operator
confirmation of present-day access, aircraft suitability, detailed operating
envelopes, and locations that are not publicly documented.
