## Location decisions and source conflicts

- **Liquid Robotics:** Current contact content still lists 460 Herndon Parkway, but the [May 7, 2026 announcement](https://www.liquid-robotics.com/resource/liquid-robotics-relocation-jessup-maryland/) schedules headquarters and manufacturing relocation to Jessup, Maryland by the end of 2026. The profile explicitly exposes this transition. A future review must determine whether any Virginia operation remains; this addition should not be treated as a permanent Virginia manufacturing commitment.
- **NAL Technologies:** [Prince William County GIS](https://www.pwcva.gov/assets/documents/doit/MasterLetter062519.doc.pdf), specifically the map on PDF page 5, identifies 9385 Discovery Boulevard and 11100 Endeavor Court as the same building under the 2019 address reassignment. The current [Naltec website](https://naltec.com/) supplies the company name, office address and uncrewed-system applications. Esri matched the current address directly; the less precise Census interpolation of the former street address was rejected.
- **MTSI:** The [April 2026 headquarters relocation](https://www.mtsi-va.com/mtsi-announces-relocation-of-corporate-headquarters/) supersedes the older Shawnee Road listing on the contact page. The profile uses 6361 Walker Lane.
- **Iridium:** The [current production contact page](https://www.iridium.com/company/contact) lists 1676 International Drive. Older reports and a testing-site copy retained the prior Tysons Boulevard address. The Virginia point is a corporate office; a cited BVLOS trial took place in California.
- **Booz Allen Hamilton:** The current McLean office is distinct from the [announced Reston headquarters scheduled to open in fall 2027](https://newsroom.boozallen.com/news-releases/news-release-details/booz-allen-relocate-global-headquarters-reston-virginia). The future site was not added as an operating asset.
- **Bihrle:** Direct web retrieval repeatedly timed out, but indexed full official [DSix product content](https://bihrle.com/dsix/) and [legal/address content](https://bihrle.com/legal/) were available for review. Current published UAV simulation capabilities support the profile. The SBIR portfolio is explicitly historical context, not evidence that an award remains active.
- **NOVA Fab Lab:** The expired 2024 calendar event was replaced by the [current tutoring directory](https://www.nvcc.edu/student-resources/tutoring/index.html), which gives Trailside Building’s street address. The lab page independently identifies Trailside as its building.
- **HDT:** The current [robotics site](https://www.hdtrobotics.com/) lists the Fredericksburg address and products. The [2026 news](https://www.hdtglobal.com/news/) explains the BLADE branding; it is not counted separately from HDT Robotics.
- **THRYVE:** The former Jewett Automation website redirects to [THRYVE](https://thryve.co/). One organization is counted. Its industrial robotics and machine integration are supporting capabilities; no unmanned-vehicle product is inferred.
- **Moog and Kollmorgen:** Reviewed sources connect their products to unmanned platforms and publish Virginia offices. Moog lists two Blacksburg buildings without assigning the actuator production line to one. Neither the precise internal production building nor the location of every global software team is inferred.
- **PerimeterAI:** The [August 2026 Oregon UAS Accelerator cohort announcement](https://proveninpendleton.com/blogs/news/oregon-uas-accelerator-welcomes-17-startups-to-its-fall-2026-cohort) identifies Haymarket. The [company page](https://perimeteraigroup.com/company/) documents its acoustic-sensing work and contact email. No public Virginia street address was found. The Oregon accelerator address is not reused as the company location, and no Haymarket center pin is supplied.
- **Electra:** The [current company website](https://electra.aero/) identifies its Manassas headquarters and aircraft development. Its announced Ohio production plant is not mapped as Virginia manufacturing. Advanced air mobility remains a supporting classification; aircraft certification or unmanned operation is not assumed.

All 24 points are **site approximations**, including Esri PointAddress matches. Census locations use address-range interpolation; neither source establishes a surveyed entrance, unit interior, test field, or access permission. Published suite numbers are preserved as contact information. Small service providers' public business-contact addresses and shared offices are labeled as contact locations, not public flight areas. No new exact military/federal points were created.
