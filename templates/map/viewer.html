Sources & disclaimer

Use limitations: This map supports ecosystem research and planning. Counts and percentages describe the current documented inventory, so a low or zero count does not establish that a real-world capability is absent. The map is not navigation, flight authorization, facility-access, security, or operational guidance. Check current FAA sources, NOTAMs, TFRs, charts, site owners, and applicable rules before making operational decisions. Locations may be generalized, records may change, and potential MPZ tracts are not federal designations.

Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb (2025).

Ecosystem regions: COSOLVE working analytical groupings derived from Virginia regional organizations.

Potential MPZ tracts: U.S. Census Bureau geography cross-referenced with public information from MARAD, the Port of Virginia, and the U.S. Navy.

Asset points: Public institutional and government sources listed on each asset profile. Locations may be exact, approximate, or generalized.

Heliports: FAA Airports Feature Service records for operational private-use heliports.

Drone airspace: FAA UAS Facility Map, Class Airspace, Special Use Airspace, and National Security UAS Flight Restrictions feature services, retrieved August 12, 2026.

UAS test facilities: Published specifications from the Virginia Spaceport Authority, NASA Langley Research Center, and Virginia Tech.
