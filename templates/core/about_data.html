What is included

Records cover companies, research groups, facilities, infrastructure, workforce programs, public initiatives, operating environments, and generalized defense assets with documented relevance to unmanned or autonomous systems.

Evidence standard

Every published record includes at least one public source and an explanation of its unmanned-systems relevance. Source-backed records remain clearly marked as pending until a publisher completes editorial review.

Location precision

Published airport coordinates are labeled exact. Other pins identify a documented public street address, campus, terminal, installation entry, or administrative point and are labeled site or campus. Region-wide entities without a single public site are listed without a map pin. A point does not represent permission to operate an unmanned system.

Corrections and additions

Organizations and community members can flag an inaccurate record or suggest a missing asset. Submissions are held for staff review and do not change published records automatically.
